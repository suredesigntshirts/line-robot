# Archive — history, not status

Everything under this directory is HISTORY from earlier phases (the 2026-06 sprint, plans 00–24,
skill-hardening logs, spike findings, retired review skills and workflows, the one-shot deploy script,
raw moodboard captures, bot-era research). Bodies are kept verbatim and were never
edited after archiving, so their paths, status lines and instructions describe the moment they were
written — do not treat any statement in them as current. What is true now lives in `STATUS.md`;
what was decided lives in `DECISIONS.md`; how to do things lives in `handbook/runbooks/`.
Index of the archived plans and their outcomes: `plans/README.md` in this directory.
