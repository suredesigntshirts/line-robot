## Who we are

**Sapdee** (ทรัพย์ดี, "good property") is a property marketplace starting in Chiang Mai and the North. It is currently in **beta**. We are not a brokerage, we charge no commission, and we are never a party to any sale. We are simply the place where listings from real owners and brokers come together in a form you can check.

## Why Sapdee exists

Every Thai property portal shares the same top complaints: **fake listings, duplicates, sold properties that never come down, and prices nobody trusts**. Meanwhile, the real dealflow in the North happens in LINE groups of brokers and owners, not on websites.

So Sapdee is designed to work **where the deals already happen**:

- **Collected from LINE groups** — our bot reads listings in groups that invite it and turns them into structured data (price, location, land size, title deed, photos)
- **Owners confirm before anything goes public** — a listing appears on the site only after the poster reviews it and opts in
- **Always a source** — every listing records who posted it, from which group and when; duplicates are matched and filtered out
- **Talk on LINE** — no contact forms, no email; interested buyers tap straight into a chat with the poster

## What we stand by

1. **A price is an asking price**, not an appraisal and not a market value. We show what the poster stated and say clearly when it is negotiable
2. **Disclose what matters before a deposit** — title-deed type and transfer restrictions, seller-reported flood history, whether a property is a bank asset or a court auction
3. **Private by default** — a poster's phone number and contact details are never shown on the site
4. **Thai units and Thai language** — rai, ngan and square wah, and the vocabulary people actually use when buying a home

## Beta

We are testing with broker and owner groups in Chiang Mai. During this period some listings on the site may be sample data used to test the display. If you find something wrong or want a listing removed, [contact us on LINE](/en/contact).
