## For sellers and landlords

There is no long listing form on Sapdee. It runs on the LINE you already use.

### 1. Post in your LINE group as usual

Invite the **Sapdee** bot into your broker or trading group (or send a listing to the bot directly in a 1:1 chat), then post the way you always do — text, photos, a map pin, the title deed. The bot reads it and turns it into a structured listing automatically.

When the bot joins a group it first tells members what it collects, why, and how to opt out, as required by Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

### 2. Review and confirm

Once the listing is ready you get a private message with a **Review listing** link. Open it, fix anything that is wrong, and choose:

- **Publish publicly** — the listing appears on the website, is indexed by Google, and gets a shareable link
- **Keep it group-only** — visible only to members of the original group, never public

Nothing is published without you opting in yourself.

### 3. Buyers message you on LINE

Interested buyers tap **Chat on LINE** on the listing page and talk to you directly. Your phone number is never shown on the site. You manage listings, book viewings and keep follow-up notes from the app inside LINE.

## For buyers and tenants

1. **Search** by property type, price range, district, or "near me" — every listing you see has been confirmed by its poster
2. **Read what matters** — every listing page shows the title-deed type, the asking price, and cautions such as bank-asset provenance or foreign-ownership limits
3. **Chat on LINE** with the poster directly. We are not in the middle

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it really free?**
Free for posters and buyers during the beta. We charge no commission and are never a party to a sale.

**Who can see my listing?**
By default only members of the group you posted in. It becomes public only when you choose "Publish publicly", and you can switch it back at any time.

**Can I remove a listing?**
Immediately, from "My listings" in the app, or by [messaging us on LINE](/en/contact).

**Why hasn't my listing appeared?**
Listings with missing essentials (no price, or a title-deed type that cannot be transferred) go to a human review queue first. You can add the missing details from the review page.

**What does the bot collect?**
The messages and photos posted in the group, the poster's LINE display name, and the time of posting. Details are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).
