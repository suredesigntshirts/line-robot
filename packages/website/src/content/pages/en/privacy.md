This policy explains how the **Sapdee** website and LINE bot ("we") collect, use and disclose your personal data under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

## 1. Data controller

The operator of Sapdee (beta) is the data controller. Reach our data contact through [our LINE Official Account](/en/contact).

## 2. What we collect

- **From LINE groups that invite the bot:** messages, photos and map pins posted in the group, the poster's LINE display name and user ID, the group ID, and the time of posting
- **From the in-LINE app (LIFF):** your LINE user ID, display name and profile picture, and your actions in the app such as claiming a listing, publishing, saving, booking viewings and private notes
- **From listings:** the property details posted, such as price, location, land size, deed number (if posted) and photos, including title-deed photos posted in the group
- **From the website:** the technical data a server normally logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited). We use no tracking or advertising cookies — the only cookie remembers your language choice

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Turning group messages into listings shown to the same group's members | Legitimate interest, after notifying members when the bot joins |
| Publishing a listing on the public website | **Consent** — only when the poster taps "Publish publicly" |
| Detecting duplicate, fake and unlawful listings | Legitimate interest |
| Providing app features (my listings, saved, viewings) | Performance of the service agreement |
| System security and error diagnosis | Legitimate interest |

## 4. What we do not do

- We never show a poster's phone number or contact details on the website
- We never sell or rent personal data to third parties
- We never publish a listing publicly without the poster's consent

## 5. Disclosure and processors

We use the following providers to process data on our behalf: **LINE Corporation** (messaging and login), **Amazon Web Services** (hosting and storage) and **Anthropic** (the AI that converts listing text into structured data — data is sent for processing only and is not used to train models).

## 6. International transfers

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in **Singapore** (region ap-southeast-1), and AI processing runs on provider infrastructure outside Thailand. We choose providers with adequate data-protection standards.

## 7. Retention

- Listings and related data: while the listing is active, and no longer than 12 months after it closes
- Raw group messages: no longer than 12 months, for duplicate detection and error correction
- Server logs: no longer than 90 days
- When you request deletion we delete or anonymise within 30 days

## 8. Your rights

You may request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing publishing consent removes the listing from the public site within minutes and does not affect your other rights.

Exercise your rights through [our LINE Official Account](/en/contact) or by typing **/delete** in a chat with the bot. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

## 9. Leaving a group and opting out

If the bot is removed from a group, collection from that group stops immediately. Members who do not want their messages collected can tell us, with no effect on their use of the group.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest and access is limited to those who need it. If a breach affects your rights we notify the PDPC within 72 hours and notify you as the law requires.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest revision date is shown above. Material changes are announced via LINE.
